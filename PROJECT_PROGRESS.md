## Master-brief guardrails

- AI must run only through the approved Ollama provider with no third-party AI fallback; production connections must use HTTPS on a secured network. School data access remains tenant- and role-scoped, and AI must not make consequential decisions autonomously.
- Preserve the specified three assistant profiles and specialized tool allowlists from the master brief; do not invent tool access or expose private school data.
- Communications are email and in-app only; do not add paid SMS, WhatsApp, or phone channels. Do not add an unapproved payment gateway or paid AI service.
- Require email OTP for every login; TOTP is additional only, and legacy remember-me cookies must not bypass authentication.
- Subscription expiry must not delete school records. Staff Loan Management is expressly excluded.
